Step 2

Get Your Access Token

Step 2: Generate Your Access Token

The access token is what lets your AI talk to Facebook and pull your ad data. Think of it as a temporary password. Here's exactly where to get it.

Open this link:

[https://developers.facebook.com/tools/explorer/](https://developers.facebook.com/tools/explorer/)
1
At the top of the page, find the dropdown labelled **"Meta App"**. Click it and select the app you just created in Step 1.
2
Below that, you'll see **"User or Page"**. Leave it on **"User Token"**. Don't change this.
3
On the right side of the page, you'll see a section called **"Permissions"**. This is a list of checkboxes. These control what your AI can see and do with your account.
Which boxes to tick:

You can tick all of them if you want. Your AI won't break anything with read access. But if you want to be specific, here are the ones you need:

`ads_read` - lets
AI see your ad performance data (this one is required)
`read_insights` - lets
AI pull detailed metrics like CPL, CTR, frequency
`pages_read_engagement` - lets
AI see your Page stats and post engagement
`business_management` - lets
AI see your business account structure
[https://developers.facebook.com/tools/debug/accesstoken/](https://developers.facebook.com/tools/debug/accesstoken/)

Paste your token into the box. Click "Debug".

How to Pull Data

Use curl to hit this endpoint:

https://graph.facebook.com/v21.0/act_[YOUR_ID]/insights ?fields=ad_name,ad_id,impressions,clicks,ctr,spend, frequency,actions,cost_per_action_type &time_range={"since":"[14_DAYS_AGO]","until":"[TODAY]"} &level=ad &limit=50 &access_token=[TOKEN_FROM_FILE]

Section

How to Find Leads in the Response

The API returns an "actions" array for each ad.

For cost per lead, look in "cost_per_action_type" for action_type = "lead". That's the CPL. If there's no "lead" action, the ad got zero leads. Other useful action types:

"link_click" = clicks
to your website/form
"video_view" = 3-second
video views
"onsite_conversion.post_save" = post
saves (high intent signal)

What This Actually Catches

Once it's running, here's what your AI spots that you'd miss manually:

Creative fatigue before it tanks your numbers. It tracks frequency (how many times each person sees your ad) alongside CTR over time. When frequency passes 2.5 and CTR starts dropping, it flags the ad. A human might not notice for another week. By then you've burned hundreds.

Time-of-day waste. It can pull hourly data and find that leads between midnight and 6am cost 3x more than evening leads. That's budget you redirect with one ad schedule change.

"Slow bleed" ads. Not terrible enough to notice, not good enough to keep. Sitting there quietly burning $5-10/day, generating nothing. Your AI catches them because it actually looks at every single ad, every time.

Sudden problems. CPL spikes 50% overnight? Could be a broken lead form, a tracking issue, or a competitor flooding the same audience. AI catches it same-day instead of same-week.

Watch Out

Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Killing ads too early+

An ad with $15 in spend and zero leads isn't a loser. It just doesn't have enough data yet. The skill file handles this automatically. Anything under $30 spend gets labelled "NEEDS DATA" instead of getting killed.

Mistake 2: Running too many ads at once+

$50/day across 15 ads = $3.33 per ad per day. Facebook can't optimise with that. Neither can your AI. Keep 4-6 ads active max so each one gets enough budget to generate real data.

Mistake 3: Forgetting creative fatigue+

Your best ad today will be your worst in 6 weeks. Frequency creeps up, people stop clicking, CPL rises. Your AI warns you, but you still need fresh video creative ready to swap in.

Mistake 4: Chasing CTR instead of CPL+

High click-through rate means nothing if clicks don't become leads. A 1% CTR ad generating $20 leads beats a 4% CTR ad generating $60 leads every time. CPL is the only number that matters.

Mistake 5: Forgetting to refresh your token+

One day your audit will fail and you'll think something broke. It's almost always the expired token. If you extended it to 60 days, set a calendar reminder at day 55 to regenerate it at the Graph API Explorer.

Key Takeaway: Most failures are "not enough data" or "token expired." Both are easy fixes. The system itself is dead simple.
