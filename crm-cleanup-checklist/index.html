Audit Your Tools
List every tool that touches customer data — CRM, accounting, spreadsheets, email, project management. All of them.
Identify which tools overlap — If two tools hold the same contacts, quotes, or job data, mark them.
Cancel or archive tools nobody uses — No logins in 3 months? Kill it. Export the data first, then cancel.
Clean Your Contacts
Merge duplicate contacts — Same person, multiple entries. Find them by email or phone number and merge.
Archive dead contacts — No engagement in 12+ months and not a current client? Move them out.
Fill in missing fields on every active contact — Name, company, email, phone, status. No blanks allowed.
Delete test entries and junk data — "Test Contact", "asdfjkl", your own email 4 times. Clean it all out.
Standardise Your Data
Pick one phone number format and fix all contacts — Use +61 format. Not "04XX", "(04)", or random variations.
Standardise company names — "SBC Plumbing Pty Ltd" not "SBC" and "sbc plumbing" and "S.B.C."
Use consistent status labels — Define your stages (Lead, Quoted, Client, Past Client) and fix every contact.
Tag every contact with a source — Referral, website, cold outreach, event. Track where they came from.
Set Up Your System
Pick one CRM as your single source of truth — All other tools feed into or read from this one. No exceptions.
Set up required fields so new junk can't get in — Name, email, company, and status mandatory on every new contact.
Connect your CRM to email — Every email should log against the contact automatically. No manual copy-paste.
Connect your CRM to your accounting tool — Xero, MYOB, QuickBooks. Invoices sync, not manually entered twice.
Run one test automation to prove your data works — Simple follow-up email or weekly report. If it works, you're AI-ready.
