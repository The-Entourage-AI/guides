Section 1
What Are Claude Connectors?
The feature that turns Claude from a chatbot into an actual work tool.

Think of connectors as giving Claude hands. Without them, Claude can only talk. With them, it can reach into your apps, pull data, write documents, draft emails, and actually do work.

Here's why this matters: 80% of a knowledge worker's day is spent moving information between apps. Copy from a spreadsheet, paste into an email. Check the calendar, update the doc. Pull numbers from one tab, format them in another.

Connectors let Claude handle all of that. You talk to it in plain English, and it talks to your apps for you.

Section 2
Step-by-Step Setup
Five steps. Five minutes. No technical skills required.
1

Open Claude

Go to claude.ai and log in. If you don't have an account yet, sign up. The free tier works, but Pro ($20/month) gives you more messages and priority access to new features.

2

Find the Connectors Menu

Look for the + icon in the bottom-left of the chat window. Click it. You'll see a list of available connectors. This is where the magic lives.

3

Connect Your Google Account

Click on any Google connector (Sheets, Docs, Gmail, etc.) and you'll get a standard OAuth popup. This is the same "Sign in with Google" flow you've done a thousand times. Click allow, and you're connected.

Claude doesn't store your password. It uses a secure token that you can revoke anytime from your Google account settings.

4

Start Talking to Your Data

Once connected, just tell Claude what you need. "Summarise my sales spreadsheet." "Draft a reply to the last email from Sarah." "What's on my calendar tomorrow?" It reads and writes directly to your apps.

5

Repeat for Every App

Go back to the + menu and connect everything else you use. Each connector takes about 30 seconds. The more you connect, the more Claude can do across your entire workflow.

Section 4
Copy-Paste Prompts
Ready-to-use prompts for the most common workflows. Copy them. Paste them. Watch Claude do the work.
📊 Google Sheets Prompts
Data Analysis
Analyse my spreadsheet [NAME]. Give me: - Top 5 trends you notice - Any anomalies or outliers - A summary table of key metrics - 3 specific recommendations based on the data
Data Cleanup
Clean up my spreadsheet [NAME]: - Remove duplicate rows - Standardise date formats to DD/MM/YYYY - Fix inconsistent naming (e.g., "Pty Ltd" vs "Pty. Ltd.") - Flag any rows with missing critical data - Create a summary of all changes made
📧 Gmail Prompts
Email Drafting
Read the last email from [NAME/EMAIL]. Draft a reply that: - Acknowledges their key points - Answers any questions they asked - Keeps it under 150 words - Tone: professional but friendly, not corporate - Include a clear next step or CTA
Inbox Summary
Summarise my inbox from the last 24 hours: - Group emails by urgency (respond today / this week / FYI only) - Flag anything that mentions deadlines or money - List any emails that need a reply I haven't sent yet - Keep each summary to one sentence
📅 Calendar Prompts
Schedule Optimisation
Look at my calendar for this week and tell me: - How many hours I'm in meetings vs. free for deep work - Any back-to-back meetings with no break - Gaps where I could batch similar tasks together - Suggest a better arrangement if possible
⚡ Multi-App Power Move
Cross-App Workflow
1. Pull this week's sales numbers from my [SPREADSHEET NAME] spreadsheet 2. Compare them to last week's numbers 3. Draft a brief update email to my team highlighting wins and areas to watch 4. Check my calendar for our next team meeting and mention it in the email 5. Keep the email under 200 words and make it feel like a human wrote it
💡

Prompt pro tip: The more specific you are, the better the output. "Analyse my data" gives you generic results. "Analyse my Q4 sales data, compare it to Q3, and flag any accounts that dropped more than 10%" gives you something you can actually use.

Section 6
Mistakes to Avoid
Common mistakes that stop people from getting real value out of connectors.
1

Being Too Vague

"Look at my spreadsheet" tells Claude nothing. "Analyse the revenue column in my Q4 Sales sheet and compare it to Q3" gives it something to work with. Specificity is the difference between a useless answer and a useful one.

2

Only Connecting One App

The real power is in cross-app workflows. Sheets alone is useful. Sheets + Gmail + Calendar together is a force multiplier. Connect everything and let Claude chain tasks across your whole workflow.

3

Not Checking Output

Claude is incredibly capable, but it's not infallible. Always review emails before sending, double-check numbers before sharing, and verify data before acting on it. Trust, but verify.

4

Thinking It's Cheating

Using a calculator isn't cheating at maths. Using spell check isn't cheating at writing. Using Claude isn't cheating at work. It's using the best tool available. The people who get ahead are the ones who stop overthinking this and just start using it.

5

Waiting

The biggest mistake is not starting. Every day you don't use this is a day you're doing manually what a machine could do in seconds. The setup takes 5 minutes. You'll make that back on day one.

🔒

Privacy & security: Claude uses OAuth (the same secure protocol banks use) to connect to your apps. It doesn't see or store your passwords. You can revoke access anytime from your Google/Slack/etc. account settings. Your data is processed in the conversation and not used to train the model.
