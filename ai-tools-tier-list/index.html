Writing And Content

📋 Template
Writing, Content and Copy

The tools you use when you need words on a page. Emails, social posts, blog articles, proposals, ad copy.

Setup tip for Claude: Create a Project called "Brand Voice." Upload your website copy, best emails, social posts you're proud of, and your brand guidelines. Now every conversation in that Project writes like you. Takes 5 minutes to set up, saves hours every week.

Setup tip for Grammarly: Install the browser extension (grammarly.com). It works inside Gmail, Google Docs, LinkedIn, Slack, everywhere. Even the free version catches things you'll miss.

Key Takeaway: Claude for quality. Grammarly for polish. That's the $32/month combo that replaces a copywriter for 80% of tasks.

CRM And Sales

📋 Template
CRM, Sales and Lead Management

The tools that track your leads, manage your pipeline, and help you close more deals.

Setup tip for HubSpot: The free CRM is genuinely useful. Don't pay for anything until you've outgrown the free plan. Import your contacts, set up your pipeline stages, turn on the AI lead scoring. That alone puts you ahead of most businesses.

Setup tip for GHL: If you're already using GHL, connect it to Zapier or n8n. Auto-tag leads based on their form answers. Auto-move them through pipeline stages based on activity. Saves your sales team hours of manual data entry.

Key Takeaway: HubSpot free CRM is the best starting point. GHL if you want everything in one tool. Add Clay when you're ready to do serious outbound.

Project Management

📋 Template
Project Management and Team Productivity

The tools that keep your team organised, your tasks tracked, and your knowledge in one place.

Setup tip for Notion: Create three databases: Tasks, SOPs, and Meeting Notes. Connect them with relations. Turn on Notion AI. Now you can ask "What were the action items from last Tuesday's meeting?" and it'll pull them up. That alone is worth the subscription.

Google Sheets alternative: Don't sleep on Google Sheets for project management. It's free, everyone knows how to use it, and with Google Gemini built in, you can ask questions about your data in plain English. We track our entire content pipeline in Sheets.

Key Takeaway: Notion if you want the AI features. Google Sheets if you want free and simple. Both work. Pick one and actually use it.

Customer Support

📋 Template
Customer Support and Communication

The tools that handle customer messages so your team can focus on the complex stuff.

Setup tip for Missive: Set up AI rules. Example: "If a customer email contains the words 'refund' or 'cancel', auto-assign to senior support and flag as urgent." These rules take 2 minutes to create and catch problems before they escalate.

Key Takeaway: Missive for small teams managing multiple channels. Intercom or Freshdesk when you need AI to handle volume. Dialpad if you're phone-heavy.

Research And Data

📋 Template
Research, Data and Analytics

The tools that find information, analyse data, and give you insights without drowning in spreadsheets.

Setup tip for Gemini in Sheets: Open any Google Sheet. Click the Gemini icon in the sidebar (or go to Extensions > Gemini). Ask it: "Summarise the trends in this data" or "Write a formula that calculates the average of column B where column A says 'Posted'." It writes the formula for you.

Setup tip for Browse AI: Point it at a competitor's pricing page. Set it to scrape weekly. It'll email you whenever prices change. Takes 5 minutes to set up, runs forever.

Key Takeaway: Gemini for day-to-day data questions (it's probably free with your Google plan). Browse AI for competitor monitoring. Julius for making sense of spreadsheets.

Google Gemini Built into Google Suite. Ask questions about your Sheets data, summarise Docs, analyse trends Included with Workspace Beginner Businesses already on Google

Design And Video

📋 Template
Design, Video and Creative

The tools for visual content. Social media graphics, presentations, video editing, thumbnails.

Setup tip for Descript: Import your video. Click "Remove filler words" and "Remove silences." It does in 2 minutes what takes an editor 2 hours. Even if you have an editor, this pre-processing step saves them massive time.

Setup tip for Opus Clip: Upload a YouTube video URL. It auto-generates 10-15 short clips ranked by "virality score." Pick the top 3, download, post. One long video becomes a week of short-form content.

Key Takeaway: Canva for graphics. Descript for video editing. Opus Clip for repurposing. These three cover 90% of creative needs for under $75/month total.
